 🏥 Digital Health Record Management System for Migrant Workers  
Smart India Hackathon 2025 | PS ID: 25083 | Theme: MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech

---

📌 Problem Statement  
Kerala hosts a large migrant workforce, but their healthcare access is fragmented. Migrants lack **portable and continuous digital health records** when moving across hospitals or states.  
This results in:  
- Misdiagnosis & repeated medical tests  
- Delayed treatment and inequality in healthcare  
- Higher risk of infectious disease spread  
- Challenges in disease surveillance for the government  

Our solution aligns with SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities)** by ensuring inclusive and portable healthcare access.

---

💡 Proposed Solution  
A multilingual, portable, and secure digital health record system tailored for Kerala’s migrant population.  

Key Features:  
- Unique **QR-based Health ID** for every worker  
- Digital storage of medical history, prescriptions, and vaccinations  
- **Multilingual interface** (Hindi, Bengali, Odia, Malayalam, English)  
- **Offline-to-online sync** for remote areas  
- Secure, encrypted cloud storage  
- Real-time **disease surveillance dashboard** for government  

---

 🛠️ Tech Stack  

Frontend (Mobile App): React Native (Android/iOS)  
Backend: Node.js + Express  
Database: PostgreSQL (AES-256 encryption)  
Hosting: AWS / NIC Cloud  
APIs: 
- ABDM APIs (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission)  
- Google Translate API (multilingual support)  

---

🔄 System Workflow  

1. Migrant Registration → Unique QR Health ID generated  
2. Doctor Consultation → Doctor scans QR, retrieves records  
3. Health Record Update → Prescription, vaccination, diagnosis updated  
4. Secure Cloud Sync → Data stored & encrypted in PostgreSQL  
5. Admin Dashboard → Govt. officials monitor disease trends & outbreaks  

---

 📊 System Architecture  

Devices:
- 📱 Migrant Worker App (Mobile)  
- 📱 Doctor/Hospital App (Mobile/Tablet)  
- 🖥️ Admin Dashboard (Web/Desktop)  
- ☁️ Secure Cloud Database  

Arrows: Migrant ↔ Cloud, Doctor ↔ Cloud, Admin ↔ Cloud  

---

✅ Feasibility & Risks  

Feasibility:
- Integration with **eHealth Kerala**  
- Open APIs + scalable cloud hosting  
- Strong government backing  

Risks & Mitigation: 
- Data Privacy → End-to-end encryption, consent-based sharing  
- Low Digital Literacy → Voice-enabled features, hospital kiosks  
- Migrant Mobility → Portable QR ID valid across facilities  

---

🌍 Impact & Benefits  

- Migrants:Equal & timely healthcare access  
- Doctors: Faster, accurate treatment with past history  
- Government: Real-time disease surveillance & early outbreak detection  
- Community: Safer healthcare ecosystem, reduced infectious spread  



🚀 Roadmap  

Phase 1 (Hackathon):** MVP → QR Health ID, secure DB, multilingual app  
Phase 2 (Pilot in Kerala):** Integration with eHealth Kerala & ABDM  
Phase 3 (Nationwide Scale):** Pan-India rollout with advanced AI & blockchain
